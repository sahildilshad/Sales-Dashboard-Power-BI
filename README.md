# 📊 Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales performance using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into sales, profit, customer orders, product performance, and regional performance.

The main objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that help stakeholders understand business performance and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Monitor monthly sales trends.
- Identify the highest-performing product categories.
- Compare sales performance across different regions.
- Identify the top 10 products based on sales.
- Evaluate overall profit margin.
- Provide interactive filtering for business analysis.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query (Data Transformation)
- DAX (Data Analysis)
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Monthly Sales Analysis
- Analyzed monthly sales trends from 2025 to 2026.
- Identified fluctuations in monthly sales.
- Observed the highest displayed monthly sales of approximately 77K.

### 2. Sales by Category
- Technology: 0.46M
- Furniture: 0.33M
- Office Supplies: 0.12M

**Insight:** Technology is the highest-performing category, contributing the largest share of total sales.

### 3. Sales by Region
- Victoria: 217K
- Queensland: 203K
- South Australia: 171K
- Western Australia: 168K
- New South Wales: 155K

**Insight:** Victoria generates the highest sales, followed by Queensland.

### 4. Top 10 Products by Sales

The dashboard identifies the top 10 products based on their sales contribution.

Top-performing products include:
- Laptop Pro 14 – 159K
- Tablet – 108K
- Laptop Basic 15 – 79K
- Standing Desk – 77K
- Executive Chair – 73K

**Insight:** Laptop Pro 14 is the highest-selling product in the analysis.

## 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes interactive slicers:

- Year
- Region
- Category
- Segment

These filters allow users to explore sales performance across different business dimensions.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. Technology is the leading sales category.
2. Victoria is the highest-performing region.
3. Laptop Pro 14 contributes the highest sales among the top 10 products.
4. The overall profit margin is 31.67%.
5. Monthly sales fluctuate, indicating opportunities to investigate seasonal trends and changes in sales performance.
